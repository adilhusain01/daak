# Daak explainer: three storylines

Length 60 to 75 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, no voiceover, English titles with Hindi lines where the drivers speak, music bed plus sound on each action.

## A. One parcel's journey (recommended)

Device: one parcel worked through, start to finish. Form: a flight along one illustrated route, Kanpur to Lucknow to Gorakhpur on the Grand Trunk Road, diving into each stop.

The viewer follows parcel KNP-7115 from a Kanpur shop to a Gorakhpur cloth store, two buses and two drivers, and watches each handoff get proven and each driver get paid by the kilometre.

- First two seconds: a torn cardboard parcel lands on a counter, three fingers point at each other, the line "Who had it last?"
- Beats: hook (the blame chain) -> reveal "Daak", one dak chowki line on the Grand Trunk Road -> book (the shop's agent pays, a 402 turns to paid, fee locked in escrow, payout split by km) -> handoff at Lucknow (tap parcel, tap badge, lid photo, GPS pin, 0G sealed check turns green, stamped on Hedera and Arkiv) -> signal drops on the highway (amber "saved, payment held", nothing faked) -> Gorakhpur, last hop green -> payout (80 km Rs 41, 270 km Rs 139, Daak's 2% taken by the token itself) -> UPI ping on the driver's phone -> claim.
- Look: paper ground, ink line map, rubber-stamp proofs in the brand green, amber and red, Geist type.
- Sound: a light percussive bed, a tap blip on every NFC tap, a stamp thud on every proof, a UPI chime at the payoff.

## B. The bilty that fills itself

Device: one consignment note (bilty) as the object that explains. Form: one frame that rebuilds; the note never leaves the screen.

A paper bilty for KNP-7115 sits centre frame. Each event writes itself in: the booking, each handoff, the AI check, the GPS point, the proofs as stamps in the margin, until the note turns over and becomes the payout slip for both drivers.

- First two seconds: a blank bilty, the code KNP-7115 typed in, the line "This slip is all the proof a parcel gets today."
- Beats: blank slip -> booking fills in -> handoff rows fill in with stamps -> a row turns amber (no signal) -> last row green -> the slip flips into a payout slip -> claim.
- Look: close-up paper texture, carbon-copy blue type, stamps.
- Sound: typewriter keys and stamp thuds over a soft bed.

## C. Then and now (bold)

Device: life before and after, 1540 and 2026. Form: match cuts.

Sher Shah Suri's dak riders hand a sealed pouch from one chowki to the next; every old object match-cuts into its modern twin: the wax seal becomes the NFC sticker, the chowki register becomes the Hedera log, the rider's coin becomes the UPI ping.

- First two seconds: a rider's hand passes a sealed pouch; the same hand, now a bus driver's, passes a parcel.
- Beats: rider handoff -> match cut to bus stand -> seal to sticker -> register to log -> the inspector to the sealed AI check -> coin to UPI -> claim.
- Look: ink-wash illustration for 1540, flat brand colour for 2026.
- Sound: hooves into bus horns, a seal press into an NFC blip.
