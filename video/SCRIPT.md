# Daak: narration script

Storyline: C's opening (Sher Shah's dak riders match-cut to bus drivers) with A's middle (one parcel's journey, Kanpur to Lucknow to Gorakhpur). About 95 s. Each line is on screen as large type, at most about 10 words; the same lines can be read aloud by a teammate (ETHGlobal does not allow an AI voiceover in the demo video).

## Act 1: Then and now (0 to 8 s)

1. 1540. Sher Shah's riders passed the mail, chowki to chowki.
2. 2026. Bus drivers still pass parcels hand to hand.

## Act 2: What breaks (8 to 26 s)

3. Kanpur to Gorakhpur. Two buses. Two drivers. No receipt.
4. The parcel arrives torn. Who had it last?
5. Every driver quotes a different rate.
6. Paid in cash, days late, with no record of the work.
7. No record means no loan, no savings, no way to grow.

## Act 3: Daak (26 to 30 s)

8. Daak. Every handoff proven. Every driver paid.

## Act 4: One parcel's journey (30 to 70 s)

9. The shop's agent books and pays the meter: Rs 5 + Rs 0.50 a km.
10. Rs 180 locked in escrow, split by kilometre before the bus leaves.
11. Lucknow. Tap the parcel. Tap the next driver's badge. Photo of the lid.
12. A sealed AI on 0G checks: right parcel, right stop, still sealed.
13. Stamped on Hedera. Recorded on Arkiv. Pinned on the map.
14. No signal on the highway? Saved on the phone. Payment held. Nothing faked.
15. Gorakhpur. Last stop checked. The shop watched it the whole way.
16. Rs 41 for 80 km. Rs 139 for 270 km. Paid at once.
17. Straight to UPI. No wallet, no crypto, just rupees.

## Act 5: What the driver gains (70 to 88 s)

18. Ramesh sets aside 5% in Bachat, automatically.
19. His proven kilometres unlock a fuel advance.
20. Shops find drivers by their record, not by luck.

## Act 6: The claim (88 to 95 s)

21. A meter and a receipt for every parcel that changes hands.
22. Daak.
