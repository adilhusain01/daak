# Reach

- **Who:** parcel relays on UP and Bihar bus routes (manufacturer to wholesaler to small-town retailer), then any relay with several independent carriers: Surat textiles, pharma stockists to chemists, rural lab samples, spare parts from Delhi's wholesale markets, farm inputs.
- **What each side sees:** drivers use an Android app with a badge tap and Hindi voice; shops tap a parcel to book; retailers open a tracking link by tapping the parcel with any phone. None of them sees a wallet.
- **Why it spreads:** drivers get instant UPI pay, a Bachat pot and advances from their proven work, so they opt in; shops get a published per-km meter instead of a different rate from every driver; every proven handoff makes a driver easier to find and book.
