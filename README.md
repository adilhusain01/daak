# Daak

Proof of handoff and self-splitting pay for parcels that travel across several buses and drivers. Built at ETHGlobal Mumbai 2026.

Named after dak, the relay post: Sher Shah Suri's riders handed the mail from one dak chowki to the next along the Grand Trunk Road through Bihar and UP. Today, manufacturers in UP and Bihar still send parcels to small-town shops on buses, often across two buses with a driver-to-driver handoff at a bus stand. Nobody signs at the handoff, so when a parcel arrives damaged or short, everyone blames the next person.

Daak makes every handoff provable with a tap and a photo, has a sealed AI check the parcel at every stop, and splits the delivery fee automatically among every driver who carried it. Drivers, senders and retailers never see a wallet or a token; amounts show in rupees.

## Layout

- `android/`: the Android app for senders, drivers and receivers (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, native NFC).
- `web/`: the backend API, the operator dashboard and the tracking page (Next.js).
